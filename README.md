# CivicOS — Sovereign DPI & Civilization Infrastructure Blueprint

This repository contains a comprehensive blueprint for **CivicOS**, a sovereign-grade, AI-native operating system for governments — engineered for both present-day deployment and a 30+ year evolution into civilization-scale sovereign intelligence infrastructure.

The work is organized in **two volumes** plus **73 companion documents** — 75 documents in total, ~85,000 lines.

- **Volume I** describes CivicOS as a deployable sovereign DPI platform for present-day governments.
- **Volume II** describes the platform's evolution from 2030 → 2050+: from AI-assisted government to a planetary sovereign intelligence framework.
- **Companions** go deep on architecture, governance, ethics, operations, lifecycle, populations, capabilities, and concerns that civic infrastructure must serve.

## The seven invariants

1. **Sovereignty of the principal** — citizens, communities, states. AI is never a principal.
2. **Contestability of every consequential decision.**
3. **Auditability without exception.**
4. **Replaceability and exit** — sovereign keys with the state; tested annual exit drills.
5. **Constitutional supremacy** over models, dashboards, optimizations.
6. **Inclusion floor** — USSD/IVR/agent/walk-in always works.
7. **No superintelligent unilateralism** — kill switches stay real.

These do not weaken with capability gain. They strengthen.

---

## The forbidden list

Capabilities CivicOS will never build. Per Companion 43 §4 (consolidated) and per-domain in nearly every companion. The list grows; it does not shrink.

## The standing question

Each year, leadership asks publicly:

> "Is what we have built still serving the people, the constitution, and the future? What do we need to change, pause, or undo?"

Answers come from constitutional officers, Citizens' Assemblies, civil society, and the Foundation.
