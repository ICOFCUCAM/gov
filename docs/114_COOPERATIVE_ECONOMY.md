# CivicOS — Cooperative Economy Infrastructure (Companion 114)

This companion specifies how CivicOS supports cooperative economic structures — worker cooperatives, consumer cooperatives, producer cooperatives, housing cooperatives, financial cooperatives, platform cooperatives, and other member-owned democratic enterprises. It complements Companion 17 §7 (agricultural cooperatives), Companion 65 §8 (cooperative finance), Companion 64 (housing cooperatives), Companion 66 (worker organizing), Companion 34 (markets), and Companion 56 (sovereign R&D) by being specifically about cooperative economic models.

The thesis: **cooperatives are economic infrastructure that distributes ownership and decision-making — they deserve specific platform support that goes beyond treating them as ordinary businesses**. CivicOS supports cooperatives through registration infrastructure, governance support, financial inclusion, anti-cooptation, cross-cooperative federation, and recognition of cooperative principles internationally codified.

The discipline: cooperative registry first-class; cooperative governance respected; democratic principles honored; member ownership preserved; financial inclusion supported; cross-cooperative federation supported; anti-cooptation by external interests; international cooperative principles aligned.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Cooperatives are economic infrastructure.** Not just one business form.
2. **Democratic governance respected.** One-member-one-vote where applicable.
3. **Member ownership preserved.** Anti-extraction.
4. **Cooperative principles honored.** Internationally codified.
5. **Financial inclusion through cooperatives.** Often most accessible.
6. **Cross-cooperative federation supported.** Solidarity economy.
7. **Anti-cooptation by external interests.**
8. **Plurality of cooperative forms.** Worker, consumer, producer, housing, etc.
9. **Indigenous cooperative traditions respected.** Per Companion 36.
10. **Women's leadership in cooperatives supported.** Per Companion 47.

---

## 2. Cooperative principles

Per International Cooperative Alliance principles:

1. Voluntary and open membership.
2. Democratic member control.
3. Member economic participation.
4. Autonomy and independence.
5. Education, training, and information.
6. Cooperation among cooperatives.
7. Concern for community.

CivicOS aligns with these principles in cooperative support.

## 4. Registration infrastructure

### 4.1 Mechanisms

- Cooperative registry distinct from corporate registry.
- Streamlined registration process.
- Multi-stakeholder governance documented.
- Member rolls maintained per privacy regime.
- Cooperative principles attested.

### 4.2 Discipline

- Anti-conflation with ordinary corporations.
- Anti-burden on smaller cooperatives.
- Recognition of indigenous and customary cooperative forms.

### 4.3 Forbidden

- Discrimination against cooperative forms in registration.
- Use of cooperative registry data for adverse decisions in unrelated contexts.
- Imposition of corporate governance forms on cooperatives.

## 17. The cooperative economy north star

Cooperatives are economic infrastructure that distributes ownership and decision-making. CivicOS supports cooperatives through registration infrastructure, governance support, financial inclusion, cross-cooperative federation, anti-cooptation, indigenous tradition respect, women's leadership support, and recognition of international cooperative principles.

When CivicOS becomes a tool of cooperative suppression, cooptation, or anti-cooperative-discrimination — it has failed at supporting economic plurality. Capability without cooperative recognition is not progress; it is the institutionalization of corporate-only economic models.

When the platform supports cooperative economy — through recognition, governance support, financial inclusion, federation, and protection — it earns the right to be infrastructure for societies with plural economic forms.

The discipline is daily. The cooperative principles are honored. The governance support is real. The financial inclusion is meaningful. The federation is supported. The plurality is respected.

Cooperatives are one form among many. They deserve specific platform support that goes beyond treating them as ordinary businesses. Anything less abandons cooperative economy to corporate-favored frameworks.
