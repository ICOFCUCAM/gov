## What CivicOS is

A blueprint for sovereign-grade, AI-native digital public infrastructure for governments, designed across 30+ years from present-day deployment to civilization-scale infrastructure. 122 documents, ~140,000 lines, on `claude/sovereign-systems-architecture-q4CcR` branch.

---

## The seven invariants

1. **Sovereignty of the principal** — citizens, communities, states. AI is never principal.
2. **Contestability** of every consequential decision.
3. **Auditability** without exception.
4. **Replaceability and exit** — sovereign keys with state; tested annual exit drills.
5. **Constitutional supremacy** over models, dashboards, optimizations.
6. **Inclusion floor** — USSD/IVR/agent/walk-in always works.
7. **No superintelligent unilateralism** — kill switches stay real.

These do not weaken with capability gain. They strengthen.

---

## The forbidden list (partial)

CivicOS will never build:
- Mass surveillance of lawful private life.
- Predictive policing of individuals.
- Algorithmic justice decisions.
- Citizen-scoring systems.
- AI principals (AI is always agent).
- Commercial use of children's data.
- Foreign data demands without local court order.

This list grows. It does not shrink.

---

## The standing question

Each year, leadership asks publicly:

> "Is what we have built still serving the people, the constitution, and the future? What do we need to change, pause, or undo?"

---

## What CivicOS is not

- Not utopia.
- Not surveillance state.
- Not vendor lock-in.
- Not citizen-scoring.
- Not replacement for democratic deliberation.
- Not infallible.
- Not finished.
