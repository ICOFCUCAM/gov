# CivicOS — Labor Power and Worker Organizing (Companion 66)

This companion specifies how CivicOS treats workers' power to organize, bargain collectively, and shape their working lives. It complements Volume I §24 (CivicWork), Companion 09 (CivicWork evolution), Companion 31 §5 (worker protection in robotics), Companion 34 §6 (markets), Companion 47 §8 (workplace gender equity), and Companion 48 §11 (anti-trafficking) by going deep on the operational design of labor systems that respect worker power.

The thesis: **workers are not just producers; they are agents of their own working lives, with the right to organize, bargain, and shape the conditions of their work**. CivicOS is built to support worker rights, union organizing, collective bargaining, occupational safety, fair scheduling, and protection from algorithmic management excess — within sovereign and cultural variation, with anti-discrimination strict, with informal economy on-ramps real.

The discipline: worker rights universal regardless of formal status; right to organize protected; collective bargaining supported; algorithmic management regulated; whistleblower protection real; gig economy not exempted; informal economy integrated without coercion; women, migrant, disabled workers' rights specifically protected; anti-retaliation enforced.

## 5. Algorithmic management

### 5.1 The pattern

Increasingly, workers are managed by algorithms — for scheduling, performance evaluation, dispatch, task assignment. CivicOS regulates.

### 5.2 Mechanisms

- Algorithmic management subject to Algorithmic Ombudsman scrutiny (per Companion 13 §3.1).
- Workers' right to know about algorithmic management decisions.
- Workers' right to challenge algorithmic decisions.
- Bias auditing required.
- Worker representatives consulted on major algorithmic management changes.

### 5.3 Decision class application

- Class B for algorithmic recommendations to managers.
- Class C with reversibility for algorithmic decisions affecting workers (scheduling, etc.).
- Class D for algorithmic decisions on hiring, firing, promotion (must have human judgment).

### 5.4 Discipline

- Stratified evaluation across worker demographics.
- Algorithmic Ombudsman audits routinely.
- Worker complaints investigated.

### 5.5 Forbidden

- Algorithmic firing decisions absent human judgment.
- Algorithmic management discriminating based on protected characteristics.
- Worker surveillance through algorithmic management beyond legitimate purposes.
- Use of worker data for adverse decisions in unrelated contexts.

## 8. Fair scheduling

### 8.1 The pattern

Erratic, just-in-time scheduling damages workers' lives. Fair scheduling regulation matters.

### 8.2 Mechanisms

- Advance notice of schedules.
- Predictability of hours.
- Right to request schedule changes.
- Anti-clopening (closing then opening) protections.
- Algorithmic scheduling subject to fair scheduling rules.

### 8.3 Discipline

- Worker input on scheduling.
- Anti-discrimination in scheduling.

### 8.4 Forbidden

- Algorithmic scheduling that violates fair scheduling rules.
- Use of scheduling for retaliation.
- Discriminatory scheduling.

## 16. The labor power north star

Workers are agents, not just inputs. Their right to organize, bargain, and shape their working lives is foundational to economic justice and democratic vitality. CivicOS supports worker power through right-to-organize protections, collective bargaining infrastructure, algorithmic management regulation, whistleblower protection, workplace safety, fair scheduling, gig economy worker rights, informal economy integration without coercion, vulnerable worker protections, and public sector worker rights.

When CivicOS becomes a tool of union-busting, worker surveillance, algorithmic management excess, anti-retaliation failure, or worker rights erosion — it has failed labor power. Capability without worker rights is not progress; it is the institutionalization of exploitation dressed as efficiency.

When the platform serves worker power — through organizing protection, bargaining support, algorithmic management oversight, whistleblower channels, vulnerable worker protection, anti-retaliation enforcement — it earns the right to be infrastructure for economically just societies.

The discipline is daily. The right to organize is structural. The protection is real. The dignity of work is preserved.

Workers do the work that makes everything else possible. The platform exists to support them in shaping their working lives. Anything less abandons the people whose labor sustains the world.
