# CivicOS — Civic Data Trusts Operations (Companion 107)

This companion provides operational detail on Civic Data Trusts — the independent fiduciary bodies that steward aggregated, anonymized data on behalf of citizens. It complements Companion 13 §6 (data trusts overview), Companion 35 (knowledge commons), Companion 23 (national digital twin), Companion 36 (indigenous data sovereignty), and Companion 56 (sovereign R&D) by being operationally specific: how do data trusts actually work day-to-day to enable legitimate research and innovation while protecting citizens.

The thesis: **Civic Data Trusts are the operational answer to "how do we enable beneficial use of aggregated citizen data without recreating surveillance economy practices"**. The answer is fiduciary structures with citizen-aligned governance, strict purpose limits, technical safeguards, and accountability. This companion specifies the day-to-day operations.

The discipline: fiduciary duty to data principals; citizen-aligned governance; strict purpose limits; technical safeguards (differential privacy, secure enclaves); access auditing; benefit-sharing where applicable; indigenous and community data sovereignty respected; civil society standing; transparent operation.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Fiduciary duty to data principals.** Trust serves citizens, not state or commercial.
2. **Citizen-aligned governance.** Multi-stakeholder with civil society, citizens.
3. **Strict purpose limits.** Per-project assessment.
4. **Technical safeguards mandatory.** Differential privacy, secure enclaves.
5. **Access auditing.** Every access logged.
6. **Benefit-sharing where applicable.** Especially for indigenous and community data.
7. **Indigenous data sovereignty respected.** CARE principles.
8. **Civil society standing.** Real oversight.
9. **Transparent operation.** Public reporting.
10. **Anti-commercial-capture.** Structural resistance.

## 4. Access request process

### 4.1 Eligible requesters

- Academic researchers.
- Civil society researchers.
- Sovereign agency research where appropriate.
- Cross-sovereign research collaborators per treaty.
- Public-interest journalism with strict safeguards.

### 4.2 Process

1. **Application** — detailed project description, methodology, data needs, safeguards, benefit assessment.
2. **Initial review** — by trust staff for completeness and basic eligibility.
3. **Ethics review** — by trust ethics committee with civil society representation.
4. **Technical review** — by trust technical staff for safeguard adequacy.
5. **Affected community consultation** — where data involves communities (indigenous, vulnerable, etc.).
6. **Decision** — approve, modify, or reject with rationale.
7. **Implementation** — access provided per agreed terms.
8. **Audit** — continuous.
9. **Reporting** — researcher reports back on findings and adherence.

### 4.3 Discipline

- Anti-rubber-stamp approval.
- Anti-discrimination among requesters.
- Plurality of research perspectives supported.

### 4.4 Forbidden

- Approval without ethics review.
- Approval for commercial extraction.
- Approval circumventing community consent.
- Discrimination based on researcher political views.

## 5. Technical safeguards

### 5.1 Differential privacy

- Statistical noise added to aggregates above thresholds.
- Privacy budget accounting per query family.
- Calibrated noise per sensitivity.

### 5.2 K-anonymity

- Micro-data releases ensure k-anonymity above regulatory threshold.
- Quasi-identifier suppression.

### 5.3 Secure enclaves

- Computations on sensitive data in TEEs.
- Code attestation; results signed.
- Operators cannot see raw data.

### 5.4 Federated computation

- Computation at data source; only aggregates leave.
- Cross-sovereign federated patterns under treaty.

### 5.5 Access controls

- Per-purpose access tokens.
- Time-bounded access.
- Audit of every query.

### 5.6 Discipline

- Anti-bypass-of-safeguards.
- Continuous safeguard adequacy assessment.
- Anti-de-identification-reversal.

### 5.7 Forbidden

- Bypass of technical safeguards.
- De-identification reversal attempts.
- Access without per-purpose tokens.

## 6. Per-purpose limits

### 6.1 The principle

Data accessed for one purpose cannot be used for another without separate approval.

### 6.2 Mechanisms

- Per-project agreements specify purposes.
- Cross-purpose use requires re-approval.
- Audit of actual use against declared.
- Researcher attestation of compliance.

### 6.3 Discipline

- Anti-mission-creep in research use.
- Anti-commercial-use disguised as research.
- Anti-cross-project-aggregation.

### 6.4 Forbidden

- Use beyond declared purpose.
- Commercial use disguised as research.
- Cross-project data combination without approval.

- Community-determined access policies.

### 7.3 Discipline

### 7.4 Forbidden

## 15. The Civic Data Trust operational north star

Civic Data Trusts are the operational answer to enabling beneficial use of aggregated citizen data without recreating surveillance economy practices. They are fiduciary structures with citizen-aligned governance, strict purpose limits, technical safeguards, benefit-sharing, and accountability.

When Civic Data Trusts become commercial extraction infrastructure, state surveillance fronts, indigenous knowledge appropriation channels, or rubber-stamp approval bodies — they have failed their fiduciary duty. Capability without trust discipline is not progress; it is the institutionalization of data extraction through legitimacy theater.

When trusts operate with discipline, fiduciary duty, technical safeguards, community sovereignty, benefit-sharing, civil society standing, and transparent operation — they earn the right to be infrastructure for societies that share knowledge while protecting citizens.

The discipline is daily. The fiduciary duty is structural. The technical safeguards are mandatory. The community sovereignty is respected. The civil society engagement is real.

Civic Data Trusts enable beneficial use of aggregated data. They don't enable extraction or surveillance disguised as research. The difference is in operational discipline. Anything less abandons citizens to extraction wearing the costume of public benefit.
