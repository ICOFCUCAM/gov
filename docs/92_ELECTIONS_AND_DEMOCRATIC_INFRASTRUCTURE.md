# CivicOS — Elections and Democratic Infrastructure (Companion 92)

This companion specifies how CivicOS interacts with election and democratic infrastructure. It complements Companion 24 §10 (election integrity in security/rights balance), Companion 27 (constitutional integration), Companion 13 (institutional re-engineering), Companion 72 (journalism and press), and Companion 74 (civil society engagement) by going deep on the operational and ethical design of platform support for elections — while preserving the principle that elections themselves must run on separately governed infrastructure.

The thesis: **elections are too consequential to share infrastructure with general government**. CivicOS does not run elections; elections run on dedicated infrastructure under independent electoral commissions. But CivicOS interfaces with election processes — through identity for voter registration, communications for voter information, civic deliberation infrastructure for democratic engagement, journalism support for election reporting. Each interface point requires careful discipline. The platform's commitments must strengthen democratic infrastructure, not undermine it.

The discipline: election infrastructure separately governed; identity supports without compromising election independence; civic engagement infrastructure supports deliberation; press freedom protects election reporting; civil society standing in election oversight; anti-political-targeting structural; anti-disinformation through trust services not censorship; cross-sovereign election integrity cooperation respects sovereignty; voting accessibility universal.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Election infrastructure separately governed.** From CivicOS general operations.
2. **Independent electoral commission.** Constitutionally protected.
3. **Identity supports without compromising independence.** Voter rolls maintained by commission; CivicID supports verification.
4. **Voting accessibility universal.** Per inclusion floor.
5. **AI in elections strictly forbidden.** No election outcome optimization, targeting, tabulation.
6. **Anti-political-targeting structural.** Through platform.
7. **Press freedom protected.** Especially during election periods.
8. **Civil society standing in oversight.** Real, not performative.
9. **Cross-sovereign cooperation on election integrity.** Respecting sovereignty.
10. **Verifiable elections.** Citizens can verify their vote was counted.

---

## 2. Election infrastructure separation

### 2.1 The principle

Elections must run on dedicated infrastructure with separate governance, separate keys, separate audit, separate vendor relationships from general CivicOS operations.

### 2.2 Mechanisms

- Electoral commission with constitutional independence (per Companion 27).
- Election technology dedicated systems.
- Election keys held by electoral commission.
- Election audit by independent body.
- Election vendor relationships separate from CivicOS Inc.
- Election cyber defense coordinated with CivicShield but under electoral authority.

### 2.3 Discipline

- Anti-shared-infrastructure for election operations.
- Anti-shared-keys.
- Anti-shared-vendor-dependencies for election-critical components.
- Multi-vendor for election infrastructure where applicable.

### 2.4 Forbidden

- Election infrastructure on shared CivicOS systems beyond what constitutionally appropriate.
- CivicOS Inc. as primary vendor for election infrastructure.
- Shared keys between elections and general government.
- Election decisions algorithmic.

## 17. The elections and democratic infrastructure north star

Elections are too consequential to share infrastructure with general government. CivicOS does not run elections; elections run on dedicated infrastructure under independent electoral commissions. The platform supports elections through identity verification (without compromising commission independence), civic engagement infrastructure, press freedom protection, civil society standing — while structurally refusing AI in tabulation, election outcome optimization, voter targeting, content moderation in election speech, and surveillance of voters or election workers.

When CivicOS becomes a tool of voter suppression, election manipulation, voter surveillance, election speech censorship, or facilitation of foreign interference — it has failed democratic accountability. Capability without democratic discipline is not progress; it is the institutionalization of democratic erosion through technology.

When the platform supports elections — through accessible voter registration, multiple voting methods, anti-discrimination, press freedom protection, civil society standing, separation of election infrastructure, anti-AI-in-tabulation, verifiability — it earns the right to be infrastructure for democratic societies.

The discipline is daily. The infrastructure is separated. The accessibility is universal. The press freedom is protected. The civil society standing is real. The verifiability is structural.

Elections are foundational to democratic legitimacy. The platform's role is to support without compromising. Anything less abandons democracy to technology that subverts rather than supports it.
