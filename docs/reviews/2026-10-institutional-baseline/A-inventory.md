### 2.3 Departments and modules

- `Department = { id, name }` and `ModuleActivation = { moduleKey, enabled }`
  (`lib/api/types.ts:536-537`): records on the ministry, with **no**
  accountable office, services, staff, mandate or legal basis.
- Created from constants: `createMinistry` uses blueprint group names (else
  `defaultDepartments`) and `defaultModules` (`lib/data/store/ministries.ts:88-100`);
  `federationSeed()` gives `dep-<slug>-<groupKey>` departments and
  `modules: []` (`lib/institution/federation.ts:37-52`).
- Changed by `/api/org/ministries/[id]/departments` (add, by name) and
  `departments/[deptId]` (remove) (`ministries.ts:159-167`); merged on merge
  (`:143-151`). Readers: `SetupWizard.tsx`, `(ministry)/control/page.tsx`,
  `MinistriesConsole.tsx`, `lib/institution/readiness.ts`.
- App navigation is from the blueprint constants (`apps/manifests.ts`
  `ministryAppManifest` → `blueprintFor(archetype)`), not from the record.

## 4. Notable facts

1. Routes are fully locked: 201 pages = 201 entries in `route-lock.json`.
2. OpenAPI is generated into `lib/api/openapi.json` (344 paths, 477 ops), not
   `app/public/openapi.json`; the 2 HEAD handlers are not described.
3. Departments are records on the ministry (`{id, name}` only), seeded from
   blueprint constants (218 groups); modules are `{moduleKey, enabled}` and
   the 11 seeded ministries carry `modules: []`. No accountable office,
   services or staff link — the plan §2 gap stands.
4. The ministry registry has no store key of its own (inside `__civicos`);
   the 11 standing ministries are hard seeded in `lib/institution/federation.ts`
   (named "Ministry of Health" etc.), all `executive-ministry`.
5. 43 reference procedures exist across 10 domain modules (+ referral
   template); all ship as drafts, installed on request. None covers the 15
   service families plan §2 lists as missing.
6. 8 exchange services (7 queries, 1 event); no event-type catalogue —
   CloudEvent types are the 295+ audit action names with `org.civicos.` prefix.
7. 24 authority keys; 9 constitutional slots (central bank, electoral,
   ombudsman, human rights, anti-corruption, supreme audit, …) have no module,
   register or procedure.
8. Nothing law-dependent ships: laws `[]`, rules `[]`, packages `[]`,
   permit mappings only as 14 unapproved proposals.
9. Real register reads reach apps only through
   `apps/_shared/register-sources.ts` (ministry apps by archetype, plus
   immigration, customs, police-command, judiciary, officer-console,
   citizen-wallet); presidency, senate, assembly, emergency-response,
   communications, foreign-affairs and noc read none. Every app's main
   panels come from `lib/gov/*` engines (202 files).
10. `register-owners.ts` lists `delegations` under `authority:businessRegister`;
    worth checking in part B, since delegations are a cross-domain record.
11. 104 Supabase migrations / 46 tables; plus a mirror service
    (`services/civic-api`, 8 Prisma migrations, 28 models), declared a
    mirror that forwards writes to the platform.
12. 19 scheduled jobs, matching 19 `/api/cron/*` routes one to one.
