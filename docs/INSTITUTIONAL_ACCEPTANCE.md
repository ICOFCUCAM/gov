# Institutional acceptance register

Step I0 of `docs/INSTITUTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md`; first written by the read-only baseline audit of 21 October 2026 (`docs/reviews/2026-10-institutional-baseline-audit.md`). Keep it current: a change that proves or breaks an item updates its row in the same change. It checks every acceptance item in `docs/INSTITUTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE_SPEC.md` against the test suite (`app/src/**/*.test.ts(x)`, `services/civic-api/test`, `app/scripts`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`). This is the register that plan §3.5 asks for at step I0. No repository file was changed.

**Count confirmed:** the specification has 10 + 15 + 15 + 15 + 16 + 20 = **91** items (Part 1 §13 lines 1046–1055; Part 2 lines 2033–2047; Part 3 §G lines 2723–2737; Part 4 §G lines 3384–3398; Part 5 §L lines 4041–4056; Part 6 §K checklist lines 4685–4731). The items below were extracted mechanically from those lines and are quoted verbatim.

**Status rules (strict):**

- **Proven:** a test asserts the behaviour. Mechanism tests count when the item is about the mechanism.
- **Partial:** the behaviour exists in code, or is tested only for part of the item (another register, a generic mechanism, one of two clauses).
- **Not yet:** neither code nor a test covers the item.
- **Not applicable yet:** not used. Items that the rules make largely moot (for example, no student record system) are marked Partial, with the reason given.

Part 6 checklist items ask for evidence for a real institution. Here they are judged on whether the platform has a tested capability that produces that evidence; per-deployment sign-off stays with the nation.

Plan steps (I0–I11) are from `docs/INSTITUTIONAL_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md` §5. "—" means nothing further is needed for the item as written.
