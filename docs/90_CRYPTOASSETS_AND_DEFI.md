# CivicOS — Cryptoassets and Decentralized Finance (Companion 90)

This companion specifies how CivicOS approaches cryptoassets, decentralized finance (DeFi), stablecoins, and related domains. It complements Volume I §9 (CivicPay), Companion 65 (public banking), Volume I §34 (blockchain strategy where useful), and Companion 18 (sovereign LLM lifecycle parallels for crypto governance) by going deep on a domain that has emerged as significant since most DPI work was originally framed.

The thesis: **cryptoassets and decentralized finance present specific opportunities and significant risks for sovereign digital infrastructure**. CivicOS approaches them pragmatically: integration where genuinely useful (specific cross-border use cases, certain financial inclusion contexts), regulation where harmful (consumer protection, AML/CFT, environmental impact, sovereign monetary authority preservation), and refusal where they would compromise platform commitments (mass surveillance through chain analysis, sovereign monetary undermining, predatory practices). The platform does not endorse cryptoassets generally but engages with them where the engagement serves citizens.

The discipline: sovereign monetary authority preserved; consumer protection strict; AML/CFT proportionate; environmental impact considered; specific use cases evaluated case-by-case; predatory practices regulated; surveillance through chain analysis limited; civil liberties preserved; cross-border discipline; CBDC where chosen integrated coherently.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Sovereign monetary authority preserved.** Cryptoassets do not compromise sovereign currency.
2. **Consumer protection strict.** Predatory practices regulated.
3. **AML/CFT proportionate.** Anti-money-laundering with civil liberties safeguards.
4. **Environmental impact considered.** Energy-intensive cryptoassets evaluated.
5. **Use case evaluation.** Cryptoassets considered case-by-case, not categorically.
6. **Anti-predatory.** Particularly for vulnerable users.
7. **Surveillance through chain analysis limited.** Civil liberties preserved.
8. **Cross-border discipline.** Per sovereign authority.
9. **CBDC where chosen integrated coherently.** Per Companion 09 (CivicPay).
10. **Civil society and academic engagement.** On contested matters.

## 2. Cryptoasset categories

| Category | Description | CivicOS posture |
|---|---|---|
| **CBDC (Central Bank Digital Currency)** | Sovereign currency in digital form | Per Companion 09 — supported where central bank chooses |
| **Stablecoins** | Crypto pegged to fiat or commodities | Regulated; consumer protection strict |
| **Cryptocurrencies** (BTC, ETH, etc.) | Native crypto without fiat backing | Sovereign discretion; regulation per sovereign choice |
| **Privacy coins** | Privacy-preserving cryptocurrencies | Sovereign discretion; AML/CFT considerations |
| **NFTs** | Non-fungible tokens | Consumer protection; anti-fraud |
| **DeFi protocols** | Decentralized financial protocols | Regulation evolving; consumer protection |
| **DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations)** | Algorithmic governance organizations | Legal status varies by sovereign |

CivicOS supports differentiated regulation per category and sovereign choice.

## 17. The cryptoassets and DeFi north star

Cryptoassets and decentralized finance present specific opportunities and risks for sovereign digital infrastructure. CivicOS approaches them pragmatically: integration where genuinely useful, regulation where harmful, refusal where they would compromise platform commitments.

When CivicOS becomes a tool of CBDC mass surveillance, stablecoin monetary substitution, predatory cryptoasset marketing, mass chain analysis surveillance, or DeFi-enabled circumvention of sovereign authority — it has failed sovereign financial integrity. Capability without sovereign monetary discipline is not progress; it is the destabilization of national finance through technology.

When the platform supports careful cryptoasset engagement — through CBDC where chosen, regulated stablecoins, consumer protection, proportionate AML/CFT, environmental consideration, civil liberties preservation, civic-tech experimentation — it earns the right to be infrastructure for societies navigating the cryptoasset landscape responsibly.

The discipline is daily. The sovereign authority is preserved. The consumer protection is strict. The civil liberties are real. The pragmatism is principled.

Cryptoassets are technological development with significant policy implications. The platform's response is pragmatic, principled, and sovereign-respecting. Anything less abandons citizens to either surveillance state or unregulated extractive markets.
