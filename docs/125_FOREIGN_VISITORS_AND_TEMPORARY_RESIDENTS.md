# CivicOS — Foreign Visitors and Temporary Residents (Companion 125)

This companion specifies how CivicOS serves foreign visitors, tourists, business travelers, short-term workers, students, conference attendees, and other temporary presences who are not citizens or long-term residents of the sovereign. It complements Companion 18 (CivicBorders), Companion 55 (migration broadly including students), Companion 48 (refugees who may transition through visitor status), and Companion 15 (planetary protocols including cross-border identity recognition) by being specifically about temporary non-citizen presence.

The thesis: **CivicOS serves citizens of its sovereign; foreign visitors are guests who deserve dignified service without becoming subjects of the platform's full data infrastructure**. Foreign visitors need basic services (entry, health emergency, financial transactions, communications) but their relationship with the platform is bounded by their temporary status and by mutual recognition with their home sovereign.

The discipline: dignified service for visitors; minimal data collection; no permanent record absent legitimate purpose; cross-sovereign cooperation on identity per planetary protocols; emergency services equal; anti-discriminatory enforcement; respect for visitor autonomy and home sovereignty; exit clean.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Dignified service for visitors.** Equal to citizens for accessible services.
2. **Minimal data collection.** Beyond entry processing.
3. **No permanent record absent legitimate purpose.**
4. **Cross-sovereign identity cooperation.** Per planetary protocols.
5. **Emergency services equal.** Visitors get full emergency support.
6. **Anti-discriminatory enforcement.** Equal treatment by background.
7. **Respect for home sovereignty.** Not extending platform infrastructure to visitors.
8. **Exit clean.** Records minimized after departure.
9. **Cultural and linguistic accommodation.** Multilingual service.
10. **Vulnerable visitor protection.** Trafficking survivors, asylum-seekers in transit.

## 17. The foreign visitors and temporary residents north star

CivicOS serves citizens of its sovereign; foreign visitors are guests who deserve dignified service without becoming subjects of platform's full data infrastructure. The platform commits to dignified service, minimal data collection, no permanent record absent legitimate purpose, cross-sovereign identity cooperation, equal emergency services, anti-discriminatory enforcement, respect for home sovereignty, clean exit, cultural and linguistic accommodation, and vulnerable visitor protection.

When CivicOS becomes a tool of visitor surveillance, discriminatory enforcement, extractive treatment, or cross-border refoulement — it has failed at the hospitality that civilization expects. Capability without visitor discipline is not progress; it is the institutionalization of state hostility to foreign presence.

When the platform serves visitors with dignity equivalent to citizens for accessible services, minimal data collection, cultural accommodation, and protection — it earns the right to be infrastructure for sovereigns that welcome guests responsibly.

The discipline is daily. The dignity is universal. The data minimization is structural. The cross-sovereign cooperation respects sovereignty. The vulnerable visitor protection is real.

Foreign visitors test sovereign hospitality. The platform's response shapes whether the sovereign earns reputation as welcoming and just or as suspicious and discriminatory. Anything less abandons visitors to platform-mediated harm based purely on their non-citizen status.
