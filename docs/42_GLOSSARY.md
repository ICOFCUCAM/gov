# CivicOS — Glossary (Companion 42)

A reference glossary for terms used across the master blueprint, Volume II, and companions. Definitions are operational — what the term means in CivicOS practice — not just lexical.

---

## A

**ABIS** — Automated Biometric Identification System; deduplication and 1:N search infrastructure used in CivicID enrollment under purpose-bound access controls.

**ADR (Architecture Decision Record)** — Structured record of a significant technical or policy decision: context, options, decision, rationale, consequences, assumptions, review trigger, authors. Preserved indefinitely.

**Adapter** — Software layer translating between CivicOS protocols and external/legacy systems. See Companion 33 §3.

**Adaptive Budgeting** — Practice (from 2035) where treasury can reallocate within parliamentary bands under standing authority with mandatory parliamentary reconciliation. See Vol II §2.1, Companion 14 §3.2.

**AI Authority** — Statutory body that licenses every production model used in CivicOS, evaluates them, and oversees the AI Plane. See Vol II §6.

**AIBOM (AI Bill of Materials)** — Like SBOM: enumerates every model dependency, dataset, prompt template, hosting environment, and charter for a deployed AI capability. See Companion 10 §9.

**Algorithmic Ombudsman** — Constitutional officer with subpoena authority over models, data, and decisions; investigates algorithmic harm; can order pause of Class C+ capabilities. See Companion 13 §3.1.

**Anticipatory Action** — Pre-positioned, pre-funded responses triggered before a crisis culminates (drought, flood, outbreak). See Vol II §2.6, Companion 30 §4.

**Anticipatory Action Office** — Cross-cutting body coordinating disaster, health, climate, and security risk responses across silos. See Vol II §2.6.

**Audit Vault** — Append-only, hash-chained ledger of state-mutating actions; replicated to a separate jurisdiction-controlled cell (typically the Auditor General's office); independently verifiable via Merkle proofs. See Vol I §33.1.

**Auditor General's Algorithmic Office** — Extension of Auditor General with technical capacity to audit production models and standing charters. See Companion 13 §3.5.

---

## B

**BAI (Brain-Adjacent Interface)** — Assistive interfaces that read or write neural signals. Tightly regulated; assistive use unconditionally supported, broader use constrained, surveillance use forbidden. See Companion 32.

**Beneficial Ownership** — Documented true human owners behind legal entities; transparency required for vendors, procurement bidders, asset owners. See Companion 21 §3.4.

**Big Bang Migration** — Failure pattern: replacing legacy systems with one cutover date for everything. CivicOS prevents through phased adoption. See Companion 26 §1.

**Break-glass** — Emergency access to normally restricted data (e.g., emergency clinical access) with mandatory post-hoc review.

**Business Wallet** — Variant of Civic Wallet for legal entities (SMEs, NGOs, corporations, government bodies). See Companion 17 §9.

---

## C

**CARE Principles** — Indigenous data governance principles: Collective benefit, Authority to control, Responsibility, Ethics. See Companion 36.

**CBDC (Central Bank Digital Currency)** — Where issued, integrated into CivicPay with privacy floors for sub-threshold transactions. See Vol I §9.3.

**CCT (Conditional Cash Transfer)** — Welfare program with conditions (school attendance, health visits). See Vol I §23.

**Charter** — Signed governance artifact authorizing an AI capability (Class B+) or program; specifies purpose, scope, principal, signing keys, budget envelopes, kill switches, escalation rules, audit policy, evaluation gates, sunset. See Companion 10 §3.

**Charter Registrar** — Constitutional or statutory office maintaining the public registry of every active charter. See Companion 13 §10.1.

**Citizens' Assembly** — Sortition-based body composed by random selection (stratified for representativeness) with substantive role in major policy choices. See Companion 13 §5.

**Civic Academy** — Training infrastructure in each customer country; produces sovereign engineering and operational capacity. See Vol I §40, Companion 03 §7.

**Civic Cell** — Smallest deployable unit of CivicOS: kernel + modules + data plane, with own KMS, observability, audit chain. See Vol I §5.3, Companion 11 §3.

**Civic Data Trust** — Independent steward of aggregated, anonymized data, licensing access for research/innovation under strict purpose constraints. See Companion 13 §6.

**CivicOS Foundation** — Multi-stakeholder non-profit governance entity for the open ecosystem; structurally independent of CivicOS Inc. See Companion 19 §7.

**CivicOS Inc.** — Commercial steward of CivicOS; participates in Foundation as one member among many. See Vol I §51.

**Civic Wallet** — Citizen-owned, citizen-controlled wallet for identity, money, credentials, consents, documents, civic interactions. See Companion 17.

**CivicBus** — X-Road-style federated data exchange backbone; mTLS, signed, time-stamped, non-repudiable inter-agency calls. See Vol I §8.2.

**CivicID** — National identity layer; foundation for all other services. See Vol I §10.

**CivicLand** — Cadastre and title registry module with customary tenure overlays. See Vol I §15.

**CivicMind** — AI authority and runtime; manages model registry, evaluation, agent governance. See Vol I §6.

**CivicPay** — Sovereign payments and treasury rails. See Vol I §9.

**Civilization Mesh** — Permissioned planetary mesh of sovereign deployments at 2050+. See Vol II §5.3.

**Civilization Engine** — AI maturity stage at 2050+ (AI as cross-sovereign coordinator under multilateral mandate). See Vol II §6.

**Class A/B/C/D/E (Decision Classes)** — AI governance tiers: A=informational, B=advisory, C=conditional automation, D=restricted (drafting only), E=sovereign coordination (2050+). See Companion 10 §2.

**Cognitive Liberty** — Right to determine the contents and processes of one's own mind. Constitutional commitment in CivicOS. See Companion 32 §5.

**Computable Law** — Legislative text accompanied by machine-readable rule packs that run in shadow mode upon gazetting. See Vol II §2.1.

**Continuous Parliament** — Practice (from 2040) where standing committees operate with continuous data; major matters still require chamber vote. See Vol II §3.4.

**Counterfactual Analysis** — "What would have happened if..." analysis routine in NEOS. See Companion 14 §3.1.

---

## D

**Decision Class** — See Class A/B/C/D/E.

**Differential Privacy** — Privacy-preserving aggregation technique adding calibrated noise to protect individuals while enabling statistics. See Companion 23 §6.1.

**DID (Decentralized Identifier)** — W3C standard for self-sovereign identifiers used in Civic Wallet credentials.

**Distributional Impact Report** — Mandatory analysis before any economic change above a threshold; assesses winners, losers, equity, intergenerational impact. See Companion 14 §5.

---

## E

**EHR (Electronic Health Record)** — Longitudinal health record in FHIR format, citizen-controlled. See Vol I §13, Companion 38.

**eIDAS** — EU regulation on electronic identification and trust services; CivicOS aligns trust services with eIDAS-like standards. See Vol I §47.

**Era Review** — Decadal comprehensive review of CivicOS; honors achievements, learns from failures, reaffirms or revises commitments. See Companion 41 §5.

**Exit Drill** — Annual rehearsal of sovereign exit from CivicOS or from any external dependency. See Companion 20 §7.4.

---

## F

**FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable)** — Standards for research data sharing. See Companion 35 §4.

**FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources)** — HL7 standard for health information exchange.

**FPIC (Free, Prior, and Informed Consent)** — Operating principle for engagement with indigenous peoples and self-determined communities. See Companion 36 §3.

**Future Generations Commissioner** — Constitutional officer with standing voice for citizens not yet born; reviews long-horizon optimization choices. See Companion 13 §3.4.

---

## G

**Goal Pack** — Translation of political mandate into machine-actionable goals for the platform's orchestrator. See Vol II §3.1.

**GovStack** — International initiative for digital government building blocks; CivicOS aligns with GovStack standards.

---

## H

**HSM (Hardware Security Module)** — Physical computing device safeguarding cryptographic keys; sovereign keys held in sovereign-controlled HSMs. See Companion 11 §2.

---

## I

**Invariants** — The seven foundational commitments that hold across all eras of CivicOS. See Vol II Part 0.

**IVF (Invariant Violation Finding)** — Authority of Sovereign Trust Officer to issue formal finding that automatically pauses non-essential autonomous operations. See Companion 13 §3.2.

---

## J

**Just Transition** — Worker and community support during shifts in industry (e.g., decarbonization). See Companion 40 §4.3.

---

## K

**K-Anonymity** — Privacy property: each record is indistinguishable from at least k-1 others on quasi-identifiers. Used in micro-data releases. See Companion 23 §6.2.

**Kill Switch** — Mechanism to pause an AI capability; tested annually. See Companion 10 §12.

---

## L

**Lawful Intercept Module** — Separately licensed, distinctly governed module for narrow targeted surveillance under judicial warrant; not bundled with standard distribution. See Companion 24 §4.

**LTS (Long-Term Support)** — Calendar-versioned kernel branches with multi-year support windows. See Companion 11 §1.4.

---

## M

**mDL (Mobile Driver's License)** — ISO 18013-5 standard for digital driver's licenses; CivicOS supports mDL profile.

**MPI (Master Patient Index)** — Unique patient identification across health providers. See Companion 38 §2.

**Multidimensional Welfare Metrics** — GDP supplementary measurement (from 2045) covering material wellbeing, health, education, work, housing, civic engagement, social connection, environment, safety, subjective wellbeing, intergenerational. See Companion 14 §8.

---

## N

**NEOS (National Economic Operating System)** — Continuously updated, instrumented, contestable model of the national economy coupled to operational rails. See Vol I §65, Companion 14.

---

## O

**OCDS (Open Contracting Data Standard)** — Standard for procurement transparency; CivicProcure publishes by default. See Vol I §11.

**OER (Open Educational Resources)** — Freely accessible learning materials. See Companion 35 §5.

**Office of Goal Translation** — Operational office helping political bodies translate intent into machine-actionable goals. See Companion 13 §10.3.

**Office of Reversibility** — Operational office maintaining catalog of every Class C+ decision class and its reversibility specification. See Companion 13 §10.2.

**Office of the Charter Registrar** — Operational office maintaining public registry of every standing charter. See Companion 13 §10.1.

---

## P

**Per-RP UID** — Per-relying-party pseudonymous identifier; deterministic per (CivicID, RP, purpose); prevents cross-service correlation without consent. See Vol I §10.2.

**People's Editor** — Constitutional officer ensuring citizen-facing language remains plain, dignified, non-coercive. See Companion 13 §3.3.

**Persuasive Optimization** — Forbidden in civic surfaces; A/B testing for engagement on civic surfaces is forbidden, only for clarity, accessibility, comprehension. See Companion 12 §11.

**PFM (Public Financial Management)** — Domain covering treasury, budgeting, payments; CivicPay supports.

**Planetary Protocols** — Sovereign-respecting protocols for cross-sovereign cooperation on climate, health, cyber, AI safety, refugees, finance, identity, payments, knowledge. See Companion 15.

**PPP (Public-Private Partnership)** — Used carefully for specific cases; not default. See Companion 34 §10.

**PQC (Post-Quantum Cryptography)** — Cryptography resistant to quantum cryptanalysis; hybrid by 2030, default by 2035, only on new artifacts by 2040. See Companion 11 §6.

**Provenance Attestation** — Cryptographic proof of artifact origin; SLSA L3+ for kernel, L4 for crown jewels. See Companion 02 §5.

---

## Q

**QKD (Quantum Key Distribution)** — Quantum-secure key exchange; pilots by 2035, operational by 2045. See Companion 11 §1.4.

---

## R

**RACI** — Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed; matrix used in governance. See Companion 06.

**Reversibility Receipt** — Per Class C+ decision: emitted to affected principal containing decision, charter, rationale, reasoning trace, reversibility window, appeal path. See Companion 10 §6.1.

**Reversibility Window** — Time during which a Class C+ decision can be reversed with full state restoration. See Companion 10 §6.

**RFC** — Request for Comments; structured proposal process for standards. See Companion 19 §3.2.

---

## S

**SBOM (Software Bill of Materials)** — Enumerates every software dependency in a build artifact; required for supply chain integrity.

**Sealed Compartment** — Citizen-controlled storage with per-citizen encryption keys; for sensitive data (health, mental health, neural). See Companion 23 §3.2.

**Sigstore** — Open-source software signing infrastructure used in CivicOS supply chain.

**SLSA (Supply-chain Levels for Software Artifacts)** — Framework for build provenance; CivicOS targets L3 for kernel, L4 for crown jewels.

**Source-Available** — Licensing model for modules: full inspection and audit, sovereign carve-outs for own use, conditioned redistribution. See Companion 19 §5.

**Sovereign Cloud** — Cloud infrastructure under sovereign control; first-tier hosting for sensitive workloads.

**Sovereign Exit** — Documented, tested, time-bound process for a sovereign to leave CivicOS or any external dependency. See Companion 20 §7.

**Sovereign Trust Officer** — Constitutional officer; custodian of invariants; can issue Invariant Violation Findings. See Companion 13 §3.2.

**Standing Authority** — Parliamentary or cabinet instrument granting platform a class of decisions for fixed duration with mandatory review. See Vol II §2.2, Companion 10 §3.4.

**Standing Question** — Annual public asking: "Is what we have built still serving the people, the constitution, and the future?" See Vol II §12.4, Companion 41 §4.1.

**Standing Stand-Down** — Periodic platform-wide pause of Class C+ capabilities for re-attestation; from 2040. See Vol II §12.2, Companion 10 §13.

**Synthetic Population** — Model population for distributional and behavioral simulation; never reified for individual decisions. See Companion 14 §6.3, Companion 23 §6.5.

---

## T

**TEE (Trusted Execution Environment)** — Hardware-protected execution environment; used for confidential computing.

**Tier 1/2/3 (Sovereign LLM)** — Tier 1: sovereign foundation models; Tier 2: sovereign domain models; Tier 3: contracted frontier under sovereign data agreements. See Companion 18 §1.

**Tier 1/2/3 (Languages)** — Tier 1: operational parity; Tier 2: functional coverage; Tier 3: community stewardship. See Companion 25 §2.

**Tier 0/1/2/3/4 (Service)** — Service availability tiers from 99.99% (national critical) to 99.0% (internal back-office). See Companion 05 §1.

**Treasury Single Account (TSA)** — Central bank account with sub-ledgers per ministry, program, project. See Vol I §9.1.

**Tripwires** — Automatic capability degradations or pauses on detected harm signals. See Companion 16 §10, Companion 30 §17.

**Twin Authority** — Constitutional or statutory body responsible for National Digital Twin governance. See Companion 23 §4.1.

---

## U

**UNCRC** — UN Convention on the Rights of the Child; informs CivicOS children's design.

**UNDRIP** — UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples; informs CivicOS indigenous engagement. See Companion 36 §2.1.

**Universal Service Standard** — Commitment per service: accessibility, plain language, time-bounded, transparent, free of dark patterns, recourse-equipped, bias-audited. See Companion 22 §2.2.

**USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data)** — Feature-phone protocol enabling Civic Wallet on every phone; inclusion floor element.

---

## V

**Verifiable Credential (VC)** — W3C standard for cryptographically verifiable claims; used for diplomas, licenses, identity attributes.

---

## W

**WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines)** — W3C accessibility standard; CivicOS conforms to current AA at minimum.

**Whistleblower Channel** — Cryptographically anonymous reporting infrastructure for corruption, misconduct, harm. See Companion 21 §13.

**Wallet Authority** — Constitutional or statutory body responsible for wallet conformance certification and ecosystem health. See Companion 17 §10.

**WORM (Write Once Read Many)** — Storage immutable after write; used for backups and audit logs.

---

## X

**X-Road** — Estonia's data exchange backbone; CivicBus follows X-Road-style federated mesh pattern.
