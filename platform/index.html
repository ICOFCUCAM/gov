CivicOS — Platform Prototypes

Deployable static prototypes of the four primary surfaces: citizen wallet, officer console, ministry control, and the NCCC operations wall. No build step, no tracking, no third-party scripts. Doctrine alignment per Companion 158: humans govern, institutions govern, constitutions govern, AI assists.

Civic Wallet

Citizen-facing surface. Smartphone-shaped frame at desktop, full-screen on mobile. Companion 152.

  • Home (receipts, pending, alerts)
  • Receipt detail with named officer, AI Class, contestation
  • Services catalog (life situations)
  • Records (with "who has looked at my records?")
  • Civic Assistant (Class A — info only, with sources)
  • Contestation flow (three-step)

Civil servant workspace with the AI Copilot panel. Companion 153.

What's not in these prototypes (by design)

  • No tracking, no analytics, no third-party scripts.
  • No engagement gamification, streaks, badges, leaderboards.
  • No urgency-manufacturing visuals beyond signal value.
  • No citizen-individual surveillance views.
  • No officer-individual click tracking.
  • No autonomous AI decisions. AI is Class A/B/C/D advisory; humans sign every binding decision.
  • No dark patterns. No buried opt-outs. No pre-checked consents.

How to deploy

These are static files. Serve them with any HTTP server:

cd platform
python3 -m http.server 8080
# then open http://localhost:8080/

Or upload to any static host. The prototypes use no build pipeline and have no runtime dependencies.
